## Required review before publishing a successor scope

- [ ] Product owner confirms that all recorded decisions and superseded answers are represented correctly.
- [ ] Technical owners assess the dual-engine and dual-deployment requirements and list verified gaps without silently weakening scope.
- [ ] Security, privacy, data-license and qualified legal reviewers assess affected requirements; unresolved legal applicability is not presented as approval.
- [ ] Persian and English versions are revised together, checked for equivalent meaning, and reviewed by human/technical reviewers.
- [ ] Cross-references, requirement IDs, diagrams, service manuals, acceptance criteria and release gates are updated consistently.
- [ ] The new version identifies which decisions remain open and does not imply implementation or release readiness.
